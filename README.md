# Stranger Things  

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery  
**Created by:** The Duffer Brothers  

# Main Characters  
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers and a mysterious past.  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – A loyal friend and natural leader of the group.  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s police chief who becomes Eleven’s protector.  
- **Joyce Byers (Winona Ryder)** – A devoted mother searching for her missing son.  


# Plot Summary  
*Stranger Things* is set in the **1980s** in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**.  
When a young boy named **Will Byers** disappears, his friends and family uncover a series of **supernatural events** connected to a secret government experiment and a dark parallel world known as the **Upside Down**.  

> “Friends don’t lie.” – Eleven  

# Why I Love It  
1. The nostalgic **80s setting** and music are unforgettable.  
2. The mix of **suspense, friendship, and emotion** keeps every season exciting.  
3. The **character development** feels genuine and heartfelt.  

# Fun Fact  
Did you know? The show’s original title was supposed to be **“Montauk”**, based on a real-life conspiracy theory involving secret experiments!
